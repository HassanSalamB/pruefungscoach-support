Delete your account and data

For PrüfungsCoach, published by XOCHS LLC, you can request deletion through the app or by email.

Request deletion by email

Email me@hassansalamb.dev with the subject “PrüfungsCoach account deletion”. Tell us whether you want your account and all associated data deleted, or want a particular report or support message deleted. Include any account identifier available to you or the address used to contact support, so we can locate the records. We may ask for the minimum additional information needed to verify and locate your data.

Delete from the app

Open Settings → Delete account and confirm. This clears local practice history, saved provider keys and AI consent on this device, and requests deletion of an existing service account and linked records. Other devices must be cleared separately.

Data retained by other services

Google Play purchase records and payment information, and data already sent to your chosen AI provider, follow those services' own retention rules. Account deletion does not request a store refund. Any legacy recurring subscription can be managed through Google Play subscriptions. We may retain records required by law, for accounting or fraud prevention, for the applicable required period.

Konto und Daten löschen

Schreibe mit dem Betreff „PrüfungsCoach Kontolöschung“ an die oben genannte E-Mail-Adresse. Nenne die Daten, die gelöscht werden sollen, sowie vorhandene Angaben zur Zuordnung. In der App findest du die Funktion unter Einstellungen → Konto löschen.
