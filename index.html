Support

PrüfungsCoach helps you practise writing and speaking for Goethe-Zertifikat B1 and telc Deutsch B1. XOCHS LLC publishes the app.

Contact support

For app help, purchase issues, privacy questions or deletion requests, email me@hassansalamb.dev. Include the app version and a description of the issue. Share only the information needed to investigate.

AI feedback

Add your own Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or Mistral API key in AI settings. Your chosen provider charges your own account for API use. A consumer chatbot subscription may have different terms from API access. Self-check tips and sample feedback are available without an API key.

Purchases

PrüfungsCoach Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase for the full exercise library and timed practice. Use Settings → Restore Purchases after reinstalling. For refund requests, use Google Play or contact support with the order reference. Keep full payment details private.

Hilfe auf Deutsch

Bei Fragen zur App, zu Käufen, zum Datenschutz oder zur Kontolöschung schreibe an die oben genannte E-Mail-Adresse. Die KI-Funktionen verwenden deinen eigenen API-Schlüssel. Dein Anbieter kann dafür zusätzliche Kosten berechnen.
