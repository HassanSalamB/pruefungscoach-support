Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026. This policy covers the PrüfungsCoach Android app, published by XOCHS LLC. For privacy requests contact me@hassansalamb.dev.

Practice data on your device

The app stores your chosen exam, onboarding and language preferences, AI consent choices, and practice history on your device. History may include your answers, feedback, scores and timestamps. Recordings are created only after you use the record button and allow microphone access. Temporary audio files may remain in the app cache until cleared by the operating system or app storage is removed.

Your AI provider and API keys

Live grading is optional. You choose Gemini (Google), Claude (Anthropic), OpenAI or Mistral and supply your own API key. Keys are stored using the device's secure storage with device-only access. The selected key is sent directly to the selected provider to authenticate your requests.

After your consent, grading sends the exercise, your answer or transcript, and evaluation instructions directly to that provider. Optional audio transcription sends your recording to Gemini or OpenAI. You can use manual text entry instead. Each provider processes requests under its own privacy, retention and account terms and may charge your account. Remove a saved key in AI settings and reset AI consent in Settings. Revoking a key at your provider also stops its future use.

Accounts and previous versions

The app can create an anonymous service account through Supabase for account-related functions. Supabase processes the generated user identifier, authentication data and network information needed to provide the service. Previous versions may have saved exam preferences and practice history to that account. Where available, the app imports those existing records into local preferences and history. Current live AI requests go directly to your chosen provider.

Purchases

Google Play handles purchase transactions and payment methods. RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, purchase history, product and entitlement information, and technical device and app information to validate purchases, restore access and prevent fraud. Payment-card details are handled by the store. Purchase and transaction records may be retained where needed for legal obligations, refunds, accounting and fraud prevention.

Feedback reports and support

If you choose Report AI feedback, the app previews the excerpt you will send. Submitting sends that excerpt, the exercise identifier and a result identifier to our Supabase service so we can review the problem and improve the app. Reports can contain personal information if included in the excerpt. Contacting support sends us your email address and the information you provide. We use reports and support correspondence to resolve issues and retain them while needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Ask us to delete them when they are no longer needed.

Security and service providers

Network requests use HTTPS. API keys use secure device storage. Ordinary preferences and history use the app's local storage, which can be subject to the device's operating-system backup settings. Service providers may process data in countries outside your country. Their published privacy policies and contractual safeguards apply to their services. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process connection information such as IP addresses.

The app contains no advertising SDK. We use the data described above to provide the requested app features, process purchases, respond to support requests and maintain the service. We request consent before sending answers or recordings for AI processing.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local practice history remains until you clear it, delete the app's data or use account deletion. Removing a provider key removes that saved key from this device. Deleting your account in Settings removes local practice history, saved keys and consent, and requests deletion of an existing service account and its associated data. Store records and data already processed by your own AI provider are subject to those providers' retention policies. Contact them directly for requests concerning their services.

You can also request account and associated data deletion by email using our account deletion page. Where applicable law provides rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your data, or withdraw consent, contact us using the address above. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Intended audience and updates

The app is intended for learners aged 16 and over preparing for German B1 exams. We will update this policy as the app's data practices change and show the effective date here.
